Hi, I'm Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/) in Hong Kong, where I am supervised by Professor [Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I'm interested in advancing the capabilities and reliability of large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs). My research interests include:
- **LLM Reasoning & Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness & Interpretability**
